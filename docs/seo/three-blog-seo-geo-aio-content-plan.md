# DATANGXING Packaging: 3-Blog SEO, GEO and AIO Content Plan

Prepared for: Shenzhen DATANGXING Printing & Packaging
Audience: overseas brand owners, importers, distributors, packaging procurement teams, product managers, and sourcing professionals
Primary conversion: qualified custom paper packaging inquiry

## 1. Three Blog Topic Plans

### Topic 1: How to Choose a Custom Paper Packaging Manufacturer

- **Blog title:** How to Choose a Custom Paper Packaging Manufacturer: A Practical Buyer Checklist
- **Primary keyword:** custom paper packaging manufacturer
- **Supporting keywords:** custom packaging supplier; paper box manufacturer; packaging factory audit; packaging quality control; China packaging manufacturer
- **Search intent:** Commercial investigation and supplier shortlisting.
- **AI-style questions addressed:** How do I evaluate a custom packaging supplier? What should I check during a factory audit? What should be included in a pre-shipment inspection? How can I compare packaging quotations?
- **Why this topic fits:** The uploaded keyword library contains strong manufacturer, supplier, factory, OEM, and China commercial terms but almost no decision-support content. This article connects those commercial terms to a credible factory, sampling, inspection, and shipment checklist. It is the closest article to a supplier inquiry.
- **Funnel stage:** Bottom-middle funnel.
- **Primary CTA:** Send Your Requirements.

### Topic 2: Rigid Box vs Folding Carton

- **Blog title:** Rigid Box vs Folding Carton: Which Packaging Structure Should You Choose?
- **Primary keyword:** rigid box vs folding carton
- **Supporting keywords:** rigid boxes vs folding cartons; luxury packaging box; custom folding cartons; custom rigid boxes; paper box structure
- **Search intent:** Commercial comparison and product selection.
- **AI-style questions addressed:** Are rigid boxes stronger? Which structure is cheaper? Can folding cartons look premium? Which ships more efficiently? Which is more sustainable?
- **Why this topic fits:** Comparison searches are well suited to AI summaries because the user needs several trade-offs evaluated together. The topic also links two important product clusters already represented in the keyword library and can route readers to product and inquiry pages.
- **Funnel stage:** Middle funnel.
- **Primary CTA:** Ask for a Structure Recommendation.

### Topic 3: Custom Packaging MOQ, Cost, and Lead Time

- **Blog title:** Custom Packaging MOQ, Cost, and Lead Time: What Buyers Should Know Before Requesting a Quote
- **Primary keyword:** custom packaging MOQ
- **Supporting keywords:** custom packaging cost; custom box minimum order quantity; packaging lead time; custom packaging quotation; packaging RFQ checklist
- **Search intent:** Commercial investigation and RFQ preparation.
- **AI-style questions addressed:** What is a normal MOQ? Why are custom boxes expensive? How long does production take? What affects price? What information does a supplier need to quote?
- **Why this topic fits:** MOQ, unit price, and lead time are recurring pre-inquiry objections. Answering them without publishing fake universal prices builds trust and teaches readers to submit a complete brief, improving lead quality.
- **Funnel stage:** Bottom-middle funnel.
- **Primary CTA:** Send Your RFQ.

### Research Notes

- The supplied workbook contains 550 keywords: 300 product keywords, 240 country keywords, and 10 application keywords. It has strong commercial coverage but no meaningful question-led keyword set.
- Current search results repeatedly surface structure comparisons, MOQ questions, total-cost concerns, supplier evaluation, inspection, and sustainable-material verification.
- Google states that AI Overviews and AI Mode use normal SEO foundations and may use query fan-out across related subtopics. Google recommends valuable, non-commodity, people-first content with first-hand perspective, clear organization, and useful images.
- Search-volume labels in the uploaded workbook are estimates, not verified Search Console or paid-tool volumes. Validate performance after publishing with Google Search Console.

## 4. Image Plans and AI Prompts

Each article contains four image briefs:

## 6. Publishing Priority

### Priority 1: Manufacturer Selection Checklist

Publish first because it aligns directly with the strongest commercial terms in the supplied keyword library: manufacturer, supplier, factory, OEM, and China sourcing. It also demonstrates E-E-A-T through specifications, sampling, QC, AQL context, and shipment inspection. Primary conversion: qualified supplier inquiry.

### Priority 2: Rigid Box vs Folding Carton

Publish second, approximately 7–10 days later. It can attract broader comparison traffic and support product-category internal links. Add real photos or diagrams of both structures before publication for differentiation.

### Priority 3: MOQ, Cost, and Lead Time

Publish third, approximately 7–10 days after Article 2. It addresses frequent commercial objections and can become the standard link sent to buyers who submit incomplete RFQs.

### Suggested Internal-Link Sequence

`Manufacturer Checklist` → `Rigid vs Folding` → `MOQ, Cost & Lead Time` → `/inquiry`

After all three are indexed, add a “Packaging Buyer Guides” section to `/blog` and link to the articles from appropriate product pages. Review Search Console query data after 6–8 weeks, then expand the winning article with real factory photos, a downloadable checklist, or a short engineering video rather than publishing thin keyword variants.
